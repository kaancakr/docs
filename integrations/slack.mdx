## Overview

Slack integration helps teams share visibility around findings, scan activity, and follow-up work inside communication workflows they already use.

## Connect Slack from the ApyGuard integrations panel

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations**.
2. Open the **Communication** tab.
3. Find the **Slack** card.
4. Click **Connect first** or turn the toggle on.
5. In the ApyGuard dialog, click **Continue with Slack**.
6. ApyGuard redirects you to Slack so you can choose the workspace and approve the requested permissions.
7. After approval, Slack redirects you back to ApyGuard.
8. Return to the integrations panel and confirm the Slack card now shows **Connected**.

## What ApyGuard does during setup

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="OAuth redirect" icon="arrow-right-left">
    ApyGuard requests an OAuth URL from the backend and redirects the user to Slack to complete authorization.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Return to the panel" icon="undo-2">
    After the Slack approval step finishes, ApyGuard refreshes the integration state and shows the connection result in the integrations panel.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Re-enable or disable Slack

- If Slack is already integrated but currently disabled, turning the toggle on enables it again without repeating the full OAuth flow.
- To disable Slack, turn the toggle off in the integrations panel.
