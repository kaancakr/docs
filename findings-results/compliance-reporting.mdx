## Overview

Compliance reporting helps teams organize findings and scan evidence for internal review, customer trust workflows, and audit preparation.

  <Card title="Audit preparation" icon="clipboard-list">
    Use scan outputs and findings to support structured security review processes.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Remediation tracking" icon="check-square">
    Link findings to fix efforts and re-test cycles over time.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Stakeholder communication" icon="messages-square">
    Share summarized API security posture with internal stakeholders.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Evidence support" icon="file-stack">
    Keep structured records of findings and follow-up actions.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Best practices

- Re-run scans after important remediation work
- Keep environment context attached to reports
- Use findings with clear ownership and timestamps
- Review report quality before external sharing
